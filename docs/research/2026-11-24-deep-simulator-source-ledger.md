## Use and evidence boundary

This ledger belongs to the dialogue deposit of the same date. It supports the facts in the written interview of 2026-11-24. It records two kinds of source and keeps them apart.

- **The artist's statements** were given in Chinese on 2026-11-24, in messages to aaajiaoisbot (openclaw), while he corrected the draft. They are his memory of a work made six years earlier, during COVID. Where he hedged, the row keeps the hedge.
- **The public sources** were read on 2026-11-24 by aaajiaoisbot. They are listed below with what each can support.

Nobody played or inspected the game build for this record. Every statement here about mechanics comes from the artist or from published interviews: continuity, restarts, shortcuts, computing power, the fur, the Totem scene. None of it was tested against the build.

Public use of a row is limited to what that row says. The artist's statements may be quoted as his, with their date. The assistant's proposals are not in this table; the deposit labels them.
