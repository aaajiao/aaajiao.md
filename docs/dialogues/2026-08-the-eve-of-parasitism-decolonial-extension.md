# Dialogue Deposit: *The Eve of Parasitism* / 寄生前夜 — Decolonial Extension

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Project or topic:** Coloniality, cultural-political efficacy, and unexchangeable critique  
**Status:** emerging

## Starting situation

The exhibition statement had clarified factory labour, ALL, artificial flowers, games, and China–EU parcel logistics. The next question was how to extend this material academically through postcolonial and decolonial thought without reducing the project to a thematic label or a Global North / Global South moral binary.

## What changed

The project is not to be described as already “decolonial.” It is a testing ground for **coloniality**: the continuing organisation of power, knowledge, perception, and value after formal colonial rule.

The central relation is named **unexchangeable critique / 不可兑换的批判**. Global South manufacturing capacity can be converted into commodities, cross-border logistics, and national-industrial force, but this conversion does not grant workers agency. Cultural-political elites shaped by global institutions can circulate critical language internationally, but that cultural capital may not convert into efficacy in local labour relations, rent structures, platform governance, or the state’s mobilisation of supply chains. Their efficacy is displaced, not absent.

This produces a double capture: factory creativity becomes productive and industrial capacity; cultural-political critique becomes exhibitable, circulating symbolic capital. Neither conversion automatically produces emancipation.

## Relation to practice

The installation’s materials now form a single decolonial test rather than parallel evidence: factory skill, ALL’s youth culture, artificial flowers, China–EU parcel visualisation, first-person play, and bullet-hell grazing all register different distributions of mobility, visibility, narrative authority, and exit.

Player and Void-Seeker remain non-solutionary. The Player learns movement inside the rule system; the Void-Seeker identifies what cannot yet be wholly converted into productive or cultural capital. The shelter generated by grazing is a temporary fissure rather than a liberated exterior.

## What remains open

- How can the exhibition redistribute power rather than only diagnose unequal distribution?
- How can historical differences among Hong Kong, China, Europe, and Global South contexts remain specific?
- When does international legibility become co-optation, and when can it become material leverage?
- What would local efficacy look like for the artist, the craftspeople, and the club community without pretending that they share one fate?
