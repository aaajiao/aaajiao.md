## Archive rules

Move a document to `archive/` when it has been superseded, has a completed time-bound purpose, or would mislead a reader if presented as current. Preserve its filename where possible, add an archive notice at the top, and link from the document that replaced it. Do not delete archived material merely because its language is no longer current.
