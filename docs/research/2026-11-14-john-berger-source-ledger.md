## Use and evidence boundary

This ledger belongs to the dialogue deposit of the same date. It records two kinds of source, and it keeps them apart.

- **The artist's statement** is one sentence, given on 2026-11-14. It is the only thing he confirmed. It does not identify a text.
- **The review** is the only external page read for this record: Merve Emre, "The Lasting Influence of John Berger," *The New Yorker*. The web text at the URL below was read on 2026-11-14. It was not checked against the print issue.

The review is not a substitute for the books and films it discusses. Tom Overton's *John Berger: From Life*, the BBC series *Ways of Seeing*, Walter Benjamin's essay, *A Painter of Our Time*, *G.*, *Into Their Labors*, and G. M. Tamás's own writing were not read. Where Emre quotes, the row says so. Where she paraphrases, the row says so. A paraphrase must not be published as Berger's words.

Assistant-proposed connections to aaajiao's practice are not in this table. They are labeled in the deposit. They are not claims this ledger can support, and they are not the artist's position.

Public use of a row is limited to what that row says the source can support. Short quotation from the review, with attribution, is the use these rows allow. They do not clear a summary of Berger's views as aaajiao's, and they do not clear the assistant's proposals as his.
