# Grazing, the instant, and the politics of choice / 擦弹、瞬间与选择

**Date:** 2026-10-19
**Project or topic:** The Eve of Parasitism / 寄生前夜 — bullet-hell game, grazing
**Status:** `emerging`

## Starting situation

The open question from 2026-09-24: is grazing a practice of staying, or the aesthetic of those who have no right to exit? A newsletter reading (Economies of Deferral, a soldier waiting under arms) was offered as a contrast: someone who stays close to danger without being able to leave.

## What changed

- In the work, the player can exit, and death is only a number. Grazing is therefore not defined by the absence of exit, but by choosing to stay close to danger while exit remains possible.
- The weight moved from "staying vs. exiting" to the instant: many choices are made in a moment. Grazing makes that moment experienceable as a possibility rather than a reflexive retreat (条件反射式的退缩).
- aaajiao limits its reach himself: "它能鼓励人的部分很有限", but for him it "创造了一种可能，或者说重新发现了一种可能".
- Bullet-hell games are often read through Zen, meditation, and deep immersion. aaajiao does not reject that reading but turns it: alongside its meditative quality, the game is a metaphor for real politics; it restores (回补) the instant of choosing that people face in real politics, in a form that can be experienced. This is why it became a work rather than only a game.
- Assistant-proposed, unconfirmed: the immersion meant here may be closer to Absorption / 入神 than Trance / 出神; the difference from the Zen reading may lie in forgetting one's situation versus opening an instant within it.

## Relation to practice

The bullet-hell game in *The Eve of Parasitism*; grazing as the Void's temporary shelter (2026-08 exhibition-language entry); the 2026-09-24 bunker/grazing contrast; the 2026-10-02 phrase "面对现实的勇气和回答现实的勇气"; Absorption / Trance.

## What remains open

- Can the instant opened in play be carried back into reality, or does it stay inside the game?
- Is grazing a rehearsal of courage, or a substitute that spends it? (raised by the assistant, not answered)
- If death is only a number, what kind of courage is practiced where there is no real cost?
