# Dialogue Deposit: The Eve of Parasitism / 寄生前夜

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Project or topic:** Renaming and clarifying the project formerly called *Symbiosis / 共生*  
**Status:** testing

## Starting situation

The project had accumulated materials on a Shanghai acrylic factory, the closure of club ALL, first-person and bullet-hell game forms, global labour research, and data visualisation. Its earlier name, *Symbiosis*, no longer described the project with sufficient precision.

## What changed

The project was renamed *The Eve of Parasitism / 寄生前夜*. “Symbiosis” risked smoothing over unequal dependencies among factory, artist studio, industrial policy, city, and youth culture. The new title names a long condition in which structural change already shapes daily life before it declares itself as an event.

The Void is clarified as a shelter rather than mere absence: a temporary and courageous space of survival and self-authorization, given form through bullet-hell grazing.

## Relation to practice

The project extends User → Bot → Player → Void-Seeker from primarily digital identity into filmed, embodied, reality-political scenes. Its four screens organise factory labour, club culture, first-person participation, and global logistical information without resolving their conflict.

## What remains open

- How will shelter be legible in the bullet-hell sequence without becoming an explanation?
- What should remain unresolved between the four screens?
- How can the work keep the craftsman's creativity visible without translating it into a claim of shared fate?
