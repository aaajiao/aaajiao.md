## How to read a seed

Each item keeps the notes' open question, the notes' count of readings, and the ledger IDs. Suggested wording from the notes is summarized only so a later dialogue can find it. Those sentences are the assistant's paraphrase of unread pages. Limits are in the ledger. A future dialogue can adopt, revise, or drop any item. Until then the status stays `emerging`.

## 2. Grazing / staying: staying, or no right to exit

**Status:** `emerging`

**Label:** Assistant-proposed, unconfirmed by the artist. Candidate question for a future dialogue / Concept Return.

**Weight in the notes:** ×7. Readings: NL-10, NL-11, NL-04, NL-12, NL-13, NL-14, NL-08 (Algodance, same URL as IMPAKT CODE 2026).

**Open question:** Does grazing's difference lie in the player knowing they cannot exit, and still choosing proximity?

**Notes' suggested wording, not adopted:** The notes split seven readings into three cases. Deferral as failure (NL-10). Staying by people with no right to exit: soldiers waiting with guns in the Greiss reading (NL-04); in the Prowse reading, leaving the screen has become a health need, and who can afford to exit (NL-14). Moving in the seams of a rule without exiting: Algodance dancing in the seams of moderation rules (NL-08); a few minutes of "nothing happens" in *Serpent Rain*, which has no URL (NL-U03). Set against deLire's collective ecstasy (NL-12): 擦弹 is temporary, close to the bullet curtain, and promises no new order. The notes suggested filing this under the 2026-09-24 "两种庇护" entry. That entry was not edited.
