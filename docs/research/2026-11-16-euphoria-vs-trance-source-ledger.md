## Use and evidence boundary

This ledger belongs to the dialogue deposit of the same date. It records two kinds of source, and it keeps them apart.

- **The artist's act** is a selection, given on 2026-11-16. He chose this seed from the weekly seed recap for the repository. He did not confirm the interpretation. The selection does not identify a position.
- **The articles** are the only external pages read for this record. Both web texts were read on 2026-11-16. Neither was checked against print. The Portuguese text of the Spike interview was not read.

The articles are not a substitute for the books they discuss. deLire's earlier essays, Spinoza's *Ethics*, Doctorow's book, Breton, and Sbordoni's *Semiotics of the End* and *Beyond the Image* were not read. Where a page quotes, the row says so. Where this ledger paraphrases, the row says so. A paraphrase must not be published as the author's words, or as aaajiao's.

The seed wording and the assistant-proposed connections to the practice are not claims this table can support. They are labeled in the deposit. They are not the artist's position.

The weekly recap's Chinese text is not in this repository. The English in the deposit is the wording supplied for this record as a translation of that recap. This ledger cannot show that the English matches the Chinese.

Public use of a row is limited to what that row says the source can support. Short quotation from the pages, with attribution, is the use these rows allow. They do not clear the seed as aaajiao's statement, and they do not clear the assistant's proposals as his.
