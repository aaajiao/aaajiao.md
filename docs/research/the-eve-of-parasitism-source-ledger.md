## How to use this ledger

This is an evidence record, not a bibliography and not an exhibition statement. Each entry separates a source-supported fact from the project’s interpretation. Before using a statement in public text, check its confidence and public-use status. Add the artist’s own material as a primary source whenever possible.

## Interpretive propositions — not facts to cite

- “Cheapness” is produced through law, logistics, labour, platform pricing, and algorithmic management.
- A supply chain can become an instrument of state violence or geopolitical coercion when its infrastructure is mobilised by industrial policy, trade rules, and strategic competition.
- The plastic flower does not stand for nature; it is a readymade trace of circulation, replication, and transferred value.
- ALL’s closure can be read as a fracture in an urban infrastructure of temporary collectivity.

These propositions need to remain visibly interpretive in public writing. They can be developed through the work, but should not be presented as if a single source proves them.

## Gaps to resolve before publication

1. **Parcel dataset provenance:** retain the distinction between total EU low-value parcel imports and the China→EU share. The Shein/Temu split is a modeled estimate, not published parcel-level data; cite its methodology if it becomes visible in public text.
2. **Artificial-flower provenance:** file supplier records and distinguish historical Hong Kong industry from the actual objects installed.
3. **ALL footage:** record closure dates, participants or identifiable people, consent decisions, and public-credit wording.
4. **Legal wording:** review EU rules immediately before publication; keep VAT and customs-duty thresholds distinct.
